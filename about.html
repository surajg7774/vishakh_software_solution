Built in Hadapsar, staffed across India.

Vishakh Software Solutions LLP was registered on 3 December 2015 in Hadapsar, Pune, Maharashtra. What started as a technical staffing practice — placing Salesforce administrators and Java developers with companies that needed them — has grown into a firm that also takes on direct software development work.

The company is registered with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs under Real Estate, Renting, and Business Activities classification codes, and operates commercially under the brand name Vishakh Software Solutions to provide technical contracting and IT staffing.
