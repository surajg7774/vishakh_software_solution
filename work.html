SG Krashi — Smart Agriculture Ecosystem

Proof that we deliver production AI features, not just staffing — built end-to-end for a client, from the database to the diagnosis model.

SG Krashi is a multi-business agriculture platform covering organic farming, dairy, equipment rental, farm stays, an organic product store, and a crop marketplace — all in one ecosystem with separate customer, admin, and farmer portals.

Admin portal

Operate all six business lines from a single management layer.

Standout feature

AI Crop Doctor

Farmers upload a photo of a crop and get an AI-generated diagnosis — disease detection and treatment guidance — powered by multimodal AI with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) for grounded, accurate answers. A built-in AI chat assistant handles farmer queries the same way.
