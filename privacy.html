Privacy Policy

Plain-language information about what we collect through this website and how it's used.

Who we are

This website is operated by Vishakh Software Solutions LLP, a limited liability partnership registered in India (LLPIN AAF-2671), with its registered office at S.No. 209, Laxmi Bhavan, Bhagirathi Nagar, Hadapsar, Pune, Maharashtra, 411028, India.

What we collect

We collect information only when you submit it to us directly through one of the two forms on this site:

  • Fresher application form (Careers page): your full name, email address, phone number, degree/course, college name, graduation year, key skills, resume file, and optionally a portfolio/GitHub/LinkedIn link and a short message about why you'd be a good fit.
  • Contact form (Contact page): your name, email address, and message.
  • Client inquiry form (Work With Us page): company name, contact person, work email, phone number, inquiry type, project/requirement description, and — if provided — budget range, timeline, how you heard about us, and (for staffing inquiries) the roles needed and number of positions.

We do not use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or advertising pixels on this site, and we do not collect any information about you beyond what you choose to submit through these forms.

How we use it

Information submitted through the fresher application form is used only to evaluate your application and to contact you about hiring or placement opportunities. Information submitted through the contact form is used only to respond to your enquiry. Information submitted through the client inquiry form is used only to understand your requirement, respond to it, and prepare a proposal or candidate shortlist. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

How your submission is handled

All forms are processed by Netlify Forms, a service provided by Netlify, Inc. Netlify receives and stores your submitted data (including any resume file you upload) as a processor acting on our instructions, and hosts this website. We review submissions through Netlify's dashboard.

How long we keep it

We keep submitted information for as long as needed for recruitment, to respond to your enquiry, or to progress your project inquiry, and delete it once it is no longer needed for that purpose.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information you've submitted at any time. To do so, contact us at vishakhsoftwaresolutions@gmail.com and we'll action your request.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we collect or use information, we'll update this page. Last updated: 9 October 2026.
