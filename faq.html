Frequently asked questions

What does Vishakh Software Solutions do?

We started as an IT staffing practice — placing Salesforce administrators, Java developers, and technical contractors with client teams — and have since expanded into direct software development, including AI and machine learning features such as computer vision, RAG-based chat assistants, recommendation systems, and predictive analytics.

Do you only do staffing, or also build software directly?

Both. When a role isn't enough, our team takes on scoped builds directly — from a single module to a full application — using the same engineers we staff out. Our SG Krashi project is an example of a full custom build with production AI features.

Can freshers apply, or only experienced hires?

Freshers are welcome. Visit our Careers page and use the "Apply as a fresher" form to submit your degree, skills, and resume.

Where is Vishakh Software Solutions based?

We're registered and based in Hadapsar, Pune, Maharashtra, and staff engineers with teams across India.

How is the company registered?

Vishakh Software Solutions LLP was incorporated on 3 December 2015, LLPIN AAF-2671, under the Registrar of Companies, Pune. Full details are on our About page.

How do I get in touch or start an engagement?

Use the form on our Contact page and we'll follow up.
